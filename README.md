# Sync — loop tracker

🇬🇧 English · [🇮🇹 Italiano](README.it.md)

Web app to track your menstrual cycle, **with data saved only on your device**.
No accounts, no servers, no advertising, no data sold to third parties.

## Functions
- Today: cycle stage, day, forecast (next cycle, ovulation, fertile window)
- Calendar with flow, expected, fertile and ovulation days
- Flow recording, symptoms, mood, sexual intercourse, basal temperature and cervical mucus
- Tips by cycle stage (physical activity, nutrition, mind) — cycle syncing
- Graphs: length of cycles, basal temperature curve, most frequent symptoms
- Reminders (cycle, fertile window, pill) with optional notifications
- Installable on phone as PWA, works offline

## Privacy
All data is stored in the browser's 'localStorage', only on your device.
Hosting only serves to deliver the page: it does not see or save your data.

## Use
Open 'index.html'. On your phone you can install it with "Add to Home screen".
